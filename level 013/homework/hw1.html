CARS MARKET PLACE

Engine: 4.0-liter, 6-cylinder naturally aspirated engine (no turbo) that produces 525 horsepower and revs up to 9,000 RPM. Performance: Accelerates from 0 to 100 km/h in just 3.2 seconds. The top speed is 296 km/h. Transmission: 7-speed dual-clutch automatic (PDK). Aerodynamics: Its main signature feature is a massive rear wing (spoiler) with a DRS (Drag Reduction System), which opens on straightaways for maximum speed and closes in corners to keep the car glued to the ground, just like in Formula 1. Weight: Maximally lightweight. The doors, roof, and hood are made of carbon fiber (CFRP), keeping the total weight at just 1,450 kg. It completely lacks rear seats to save weight.
