BLACK BEAR

INFORMATION







The black bear is a medium-sized bear that lives mainly in North America. Its scientific name is Ursus americanus. Black bears usually live in forests and mountains. They have thick fur that is usually black, but it can sometimes be brown or cinnamon. An adult black bear can weigh 90–270 kg. Black bears are omnivores, which means they eat both plants and animals. Their diet includes berries, nuts, insects, fish, and small animals. They are generally shy and avoid humans, but they can become dangerous if they feel threatened or if they are protecting their cubs. Black bears are good climbers and swimmers, and they spend the winter in hibernation.
